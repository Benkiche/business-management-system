# User Guide

## Dashboard

The dashboard displays real-time business metrics:

- **Today's Sales**: Total sales for the current day
- **Top Products**: Best-selling products this month
- **Top Customers**: Highest-spending customers
- **Inventory Status**: Stock levels and valuations
- **Payment Status**: Paid, partial, and unpaid invoices

## Creating a Sale

### Step 1: Start New Sale
- Click **Sales → New Sale** from menu
- Select customer from dropdown
- Choose payment method

### Step 2: Add Items
- Click "Add Item" button
- Select product and quantity
- Price auto-fills from product catalog
- Add optional discount percentage
- Repeat for each item

### Step 3: Review & Confirm
- System calculates subtotal, discounts, and tax
- Set optional due date for credit sales
- Add notes if needed
- Click "Create Sale" button

### Step 4: Manage Payment
- View invoice details
- Record payment as received
- View payment history

## Recording Expenses

### Step 1: New Expense
- Go to **Expenses → New Expense**
- Select category
- Enter amount and date

### Step 2: Submit
- Upload receipt image or PDF
- Add notes
- Select "Pending Approval" for workflow
- Click "Record Expense"

### Step 3: Approval
- Managers review pending expenses
- Click approve or reject
- Approved expenses appear in reports

## User Management

### Add New User
1. Go to **Settings → Users**
2. Click "Add User"
3. Enter name, email, phone
4. Assign role (Admin, Manager, etc.)
5. System generates temporary password
6. User receives login email

### Change Password
1. Click your avatar (top right)
2. Select "Profile Settings"
3. Enter current password
4. Enter new password twice
5. Click "Update"

### Configure Notifications
1. Go to **Settings → Preferences**
2. Toggle notification types on/off
3. Choose email or in-app only
4. Save preferences

## Troubleshooting

### Forgot Password
1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter email address
3. Follow link in reset email
4. Create new password

### Insufficient Stock Error
- Product has less stock than you're trying to sell
- Solution: Add more stock via **Inventory → Stock In**

### Customer Credit Limit Exceeded
- Customer has reached their credit limit
- Solution: Record payment to reduce outstanding balance

## Tips & Best Practices

1. **Always backup** - Download reports regularly
2. **Update inventory** - Keep stock levels current
3. **Reconcile payments** - Match received payments to invoices
4. **Review reports** - Check financial health weekly
5. **Monitor alerts** - Act on low stock and overdue notifications

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I edit a completed sale?**
A: No, completed sales cannot be edited. Cancel and create new sale if needed.

**Q: How do I delete a customer?**
A: Customers with sales cannot be deleted. Archive by marking inactive.

**Q: Where are my backups?**
A: Use **Settings → Backups** to download backups.

**Q: How do I export data?**
A: Click "Export" button on any report or list.
